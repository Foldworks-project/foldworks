## Core expectations

- Respect the Product Constitution.
- Do not invent requirements that are not documented.
- Distinguish established decisions from proposals.
- Prefer simple solutions to speculative infrastructure.
- Do not prematurely optimize for hypothetical scale.
- Preserve user ownership and data portability.
- Treat the model, not the file, as the fundamental product object.
- Treat PDO support as progressive enhancement.
- Preserve a first-class experience for non-PDO models.
- Keep manual workflows available when automation fails.
- Test AI-generated implementation.
- Document significant architectural decisions.
- Raise ambiguity rather than silently resolving important product questions.
