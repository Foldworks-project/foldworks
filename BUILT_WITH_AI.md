# Built with AI

Foldworks is deliberately being built with substantial assistance from
generative AI.

This is not incidental to the project. Foldworks is also an experiment in
whether AI can reduce the organizational and engineering cost of software
development enough to make excellent software viable for small, underserved
communities.

## The hypothesis

Personal computers dramatically increased what an individual could accomplish
without the organizational infrastructure that had previously been required.

Generative AI may represent another such compression.

Our working hypotheses are:

> AI makes individual humans dramatically more capable.

> AI makes domain knowledge executable.

> AI may lower the minimum viable market for high-quality software.

Foldworks tests those ideas in a deliberately niche domain: paper modelling.

## Human responsibility

Foldworks is **built with AI**, not autonomously generated by AI.

The human product owner remains responsible for:

- product direction;
- domain knowledge;
- judgment;
- accepting or rejecting proposed solutions;
- verification;
- testing;
- project ownership.

AI output is not considered correct merely because an AI produced it.

## AI roles

AI systems may contribute to:

- software engineering;
- architecture;
- research;
- product specification;
- testing;
- documentation;
- code review;
- security review;
- UX analysis;
- debugging;
- project planning.

Different AI systems may be used for different roles or as independent
reviewers of one another's work.

The repository remains agent-neutral. Project truth belongs in shared
documentation, specifications, tests, decision records, and source code rather
than in instructions written exclusively for one AI system.

## What we intend to document

Where practical, the Foldworks experiment will record:

- important prompts and specifications;
- architectural decisions;
- AI-generated approaches that succeeded;
- AI-generated approaches that failed;
- bugs and incorrect assumptions;
- places where domain knowledge corrected AI output;
- approximate human effort;
- AI and tooling costs;
- lessons learned.

Failures are part of the experiment and should not be hidden merely because
they are inconvenient.

## The goal

The goal is not to demonstrate that humans are unnecessary.

The goal is to discover how capable a domain expert can become when given
access to increasingly capable artificial intelligence.

If Foldworks succeeds, the process should be useful to people building other
forms of boutique software.

If the experiment fails, understanding why should be useful too.
